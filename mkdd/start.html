Get Ultra

Ultra brings Double Dash up to date: add courses and cups like DLC, plus the moves of Mario Kart Wii and the Ultra menu! Version 0.5 BETA.

Where do you play?

You need your own copy of Double Dash (USA).

GameCubeSwiss, from an SD Gecko, SD2SP2 or GC Loader.SD card version WiiFrom an SD card or USB drive.SD card version Dolphin, Nintendont, or just as a discPatch your own disc.Ultra Patcher
  1. Download Ultra and unzip it onto your SD card.
  2. Optional: add the Retro Pack! 80 courses and 30 stages!
  3. Put your own copy of Double Dash (USA) anywhere on the card, or use the disc.
  4. Start it: boot.dol from Swiss on a GameCube, Ultra from the Homebrew Channel on a Wii.
  • To update, unzip the new Ultra over the old one. Your saves and packs are kept.
  • Hold B while Ultra starts for its menu. No disc yet? Put it in and you're racing!

    macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Dolphin/GameSettings.
    Linux: ~/.local/share/dolphin-emu/GameSettings.
    Flatpak: ~/.var/app/org.DolphinEmu.dolphin-emu/data/dolphin-emu/GameSettings.

More courses

Put course ZIPs, unzipped courses or packs in mkddu/courses on your SD card (Wii) or SDGecko/Swiss drive (Gamecube) to load them. They show on the CUSTOM pages.

  • ZIPs from mkdd.orgWork as they are. (If the music file is too big, the audio may skip depending on your SD card. This can be fixed by making a pak.)
  • PacksSmaller, and you set the cups yourself. Put the pack on your disc or your SD card, and share it with your friends for LAN play!
