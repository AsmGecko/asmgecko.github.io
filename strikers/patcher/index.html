Ultra Patcher

Coming soon. A free patch for your own disc, applied in your browser.

Version 0.2 BETA. Tested on real GameCube hardware and in Dolphin. A crash shows a screen you can send us a photo of.

  1. Pick your dump of Super Mario Strikers (USA): ISO, GCM, CISO, GCZ or RVZ.
  2. Make Ultra and play it in Dolphin or on your GameCube.
  3. Optional: for 16:9 in Dolphin, put G4QEJ8.ini in its GameSettings folder.
    Where's that?Windows: Documents\Dolphin Emulator\GameSettings, or User\GameSettings next to Dolphin.exe in a portable install.
    macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Dolphin/GameSettings.
    Linux: ~/.local/share/dolphin-emu/GameSettings.
    Flatpak: ~/.var/app/org.DolphinEmu.dolphin-emu/data/dolphin-emu/GameSettings.
