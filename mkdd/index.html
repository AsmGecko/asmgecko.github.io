The moves of Mario Kart Wii

When switched on, all CPUs will also make use of this.

Hop
R or L makes the kart hop, and the drift starts when it lands.
Look back
Hold the D-pad down to see who's behind you, or Y when you have no item. X and the D-pad up sound the horn. In split screen only your own screen turns.

CPUs in battle mode

Double Dash never had bots in battle mode, so I wrote a bot engine from scratch, and now you can play battle mode on your own! Add up to three bots on the Battle page of the Ultra menu and pick how good they are. Versus gets up to seven CPUs.

Seven new game modes and 200cc

Picked on the Race page of the Ultra menu, with every rule you set on top. The intro of every race says which mode is on and how it works.

Knockout
Every time the leader starts a new lap, the last karts vanish from the race. The final lap is a duel.
Accelerate
Everyone gets faster with every lap, up to 1.4 times the top speed on the last one! Not in 200cc.
Shell shock
Every item box gives green shells, and they keep bouncing until they hit someone. When they're all out, a box gives a red one.
Item roulette
Every 12 seconds everyone with empty hands gets a roulette, wherever they are.

110 extra courses

80 tracks and 30 battle stages on five extra pages of the cup and stage select, Retro 1 to 5. The first four have the full MKDD Plus lineup and the fifth has our own picks from the community. Every course has its music in stereo using ADPCM to squeeze it all on a single disc. Turn the page with L and R, or just keep going past the last cup. L+R together rolls a random cup. In Versus and Time Trial, X switches to a list of the tracks by console, or of all of them from A to Z. The stage select has the same list for the battle stages.

Proper LAN support

Every Ultra menu setting is synced over LAN and works on every console! LAN play also gets a character select for up to eight players and a faster connection setup.

And way more

Playing together

Teams
Red against blue in Versus and in all three battles! With four players it's 1 and 2 against 3 and 4, 1 and 3 against 2 and 4, or 1 and 4 against 2 and 3, and CPUs fill the smaller team. Items spare your teammates, every place in Versus scores Grand Prix points for its team, and in battle one kart's win is a win for its whole team.
Grand Prix for four
Three or four players in a Grand Prix, where the game stops at two.
Duplicate characters
Take a character someone else has, or the same one twice in your own kart.
Kart stats
Six rows of stars for every kart on the character select, also with two to four players, where the game shows none.
Everything unlocked
Every cup, class, character and kart from the first start, 200cc included, even without a save.

Picture and sound

Native 16:9
Every screen gets a wider view, including split screen and Time Trial. The game asks once at the first start, and it's a setting on your memory card.
480p by default
Progressive scan on a component cable, without the question at the start, and a sharper filter than the game's own.
Music in stereo
All music of the extra courses in stereo, and mono music has been patched to properly play from both speakers.
Course intro
Every extra course opens with its name and its maker, and every race with the game mode that's on.
Better race results
The results stay on the screen until P1 presses A, as in Mario Kart 64.

Fixes

Loading improvements
Up to 25% shorter loading times, from faster code and a better order of the files on the disc.
150cc first
The class select starts on 150cc instead of 50cc.
D-pad in the menus
Every menu of the game takes the D-pad as well as the stick, from the title screen to the pause menu.
Settings in the save
The settings of the Ultra menu stay on your memory card. The rules last until you turn the console off.
