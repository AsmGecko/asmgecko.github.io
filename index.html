Ultra Editions

Two GameCube mods you won't find anywhere else! New game modes and tons of tweaks for you to add extra gameplay for tons of fun with friends. All made for real GameCube hardware!

Mario Kart: Double Dash!!

80 retro courses and 30 battle stages, seven new game modes, 200cc, the mechanics of Mario Kart Wii, CPUs in battle mode, 70 skins, LAN play and the Ultra menu!

Super Mario Strikers

The first real Strikers mod, with new game modes, captain powers, red cards, penalties, rotating teams and the beta content back in the game!
